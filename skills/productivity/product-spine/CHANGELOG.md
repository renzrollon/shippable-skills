## 0.3.0 (2026-09-10)

Replace `tasks/*.md` with product briefs at
`docs/product/briefs/*.md`. A brief is what / why / ladder / primer for a
change proposal, not an execution ticket. Write fence is `docs/product/**`
only. Review uses Ready to propose (brief) and Ready to flip (epic).
`draft task` still maps to `draft brief`. Not yet run through the
SkillGantry gate.
